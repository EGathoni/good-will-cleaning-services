# Good Will Cleaning Services — Brand Guide

## Identity
Tagline: **Reliable Cleaning. Healthier Spaces.** The updated mark combines an architectural roof and house outline with flowing blue and green swooshes and small sparkle details. “Good Will” is prominent in Deep Navy; “Cleaning Services” sits beneath in Sky Blue.

## Logo assets
- `assets/logo/logo-horizontal.svg` and `logo-primary.svg`: full horizontal logo.
- `assets/logo/logo-compact.svg`: compact navigation logo.
- `assets/logo/logo-icon.svg`: house/swoosh/sparkle icon only.
- `assets/logo/logo-white.svg`: reversed logo for dark backgrounds.
- `assets/logo/logo-monochrome.svg`: single-colour mark.

SVGs use transparent backgrounds and editable vector paths/text. Convert wordmark text to outlines or ensure Poppins is available in the print production workflow. PNG exports are not included; SVG is the scalable master format.

## Palette
| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#0B2F55` | Wordmark, headings, important text |
| Sky Blue | `#0EA5E9` | Swoosh, descriptor, links and accents |
| Fresh Green | `#22C55E` | Second swoosh, small details and calls to action |
| Soft Blue | `#F0F8FF` | Calm section backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clear space and reversed mark |
| Slate Grey | `#64748B` | Supporting text |

## Typography
Headings use Poppins (500–700); body text uses Open Sans (400–700). Use clear size and weight contrast.

## Correct use
Keep the logo's proportions and clear space. Do not add shadows or alter the approved colours. Use the compact logo in narrow navigation and the white version on Deep Navy or sufficiently dark backgrounds. Keep the icon large enough to recognise. Maintain readable contrast.

Client names currently appear as text, not corporate marks. Only replace them with supplied or authorised official assets. Confirm the client relationships and public-display permission with the business owner before launch.
